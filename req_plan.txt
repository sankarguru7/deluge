Wait, does `wrapper.toString()` use `{"d": [{"row": 1}]}` (with spaces) or `{"d":[{"row":1}]}` (no spaces)?
`jsonStr.getSuffix(":[")` might be safer. Or `jsonStr.replaceFirst("{\"d\":","").getPrefix("}")`.
Wait, `replaceFirst("{\"d\":","")` leaves `[...]}`, then we do `.substring(0, len-1)`.
In Deluge:
```deluge
wrapper = Map();
wrapper.put("d", currentChunk);
wrapperStr = wrapper.toString();
// extract from '[' to ']'
startIndex = wrapperStr.indexOf("[");
endIndex = wrapperStr.lastIndexOf("]");
jsonArrayStr = wrapperStr.subString(startIndex, endIndex + 1);
```
This is brilliant, perfectly robust, and independent of formatting!
Wait, `subString` takes (start, end). In Java, `end` is exclusive, so `endIndex + 1` correctly includes the `]`.
Does Deluge `subString` work the same way? Yes.
