How to escape characters in Deluge for JSON properly:
```deluge
valStr = valStr.replaceAll("\\", "\\\\");
valStr = valStr.replaceAll("\n", "\\n");
valStr = valStr.replaceAll("\r", "\\r");
valStr = valStr.replaceAll("\"", "\\\"");
```
